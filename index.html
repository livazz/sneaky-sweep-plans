Training

1Tunnel Vision

Hector walks the middle lane. The blocks around him are taller than they look.

2Cold Trail

Igor keeps his eyes on the middle lane while Gaston paces in front of the lanes. Whatever you do here, it stays for a while.

3Wet Ink

One narrow doorway, one puddle, and Brutus coming and going in front of it. It is all a matter of when.

4Sweet Tooth

Brutus guards the corridor to the exit, and a box sits by the arrival lift. Every guard has his weakness.

5Who's There?

Gaston guards the corridor. A pillar stands in the middle of the room. Curiosity is a guard's job.

6Night Shift

Three rooms, a camera watching the last one, and two switches. Hector and Igor carry flashlights.

7Overhead

Every door is locked and the last room is full of guards. Watch your step on the way: some things fall loudly.

8Dress Code

Hector at the corridor, Igor and Gaston pacing the room. Something hangs on a rack by the arrival lift.

9Dead Weight

Norbert stands in a narrow corridor, facing the exit. Brutus turns back and forth between him and the lift. What you leave behind matters.

10Lullaby

Hector at the corridor, Igor and Gaston walking side by side. Something lies on the floor near the arrival lift.

Golden Wisteria Manor, floor by floor

The main mission at the start of the game. The guards change with the shifts later on, and the residents and staff go about their day.

Basement 2

The vault corridor, the vault room (the safe, a camera), the secret archives (the alarm's power box), the uniform room, the lab and the padded cell, where Brutus drags the spy at the start. Brutus then guards the vault corridor.

Basement 1

The guard room (the camera recorder, the shift's alarm clock), the staff locker room (a nurse's scrubs, a cook's outfit), the boiler room (the main breaker, the water valve), the laundry and the wine cellar. Gaston is in the guard room.

Ground Floor

The reception (the mic, the phone, a camera), the common room, the dining room, the kitchen and the restrooms. Igor walks his round from the corridor out to the garden.

The Grounds

The estate around the house, the car park and the cars' trunks, the gate, the road to town. Raymond sits in the gatehouse, by the barrier's button.

1st Floor

The director's office (his phone, the sticky note under the desk pad), the infirmary, the pharmacy (the medicine cabinet), the staff room and the library. No guard at the start of the game.

2nd Floor

The rooms of Granny Viper, the Red Baron, Colonel Kraken and Professor Void, each with its TV and phone, and the janitor's closet (the janitor's outfit). Hector walks his round in the corridor.

3rd Floor

Six private rooms: Madame Shadow, the Mask, Admiral Tempest, Countess Venom, Lady Mustache and Doctor Gumdrop. No guard at the start of the game.

The plans are drawn from the game itself by the Unity editor.
